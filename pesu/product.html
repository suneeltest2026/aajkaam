The piece

A surface left by weather,
not assembled by hand

Sabkha are the salt flats that lie between Dubai and the Empty Quarter — a surface that looks solid from a distance and reads, up close, as a thousand fractured plates of crystal and sand.

The console takes that geology literally. A single 42 mm slab is cut, honed and set on a plinth with no visible fixings, mitred at the corners so the material appears to fold rather than join. Under the top, a hand-numbered brass disc; on the front edge, your inscription, if you want one.
